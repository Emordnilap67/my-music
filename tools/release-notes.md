**New in 9.0:**

- **Spotify and Deezer links:** share or paste a song, album or playlist link from Spotify or Deezer. Their audio is locked, so Carmen Musicum reads the song names and finds each one on YouTube - the closest length wins, and anything without a clear match waits under **Find another copy**. (Spotify shows an app without an account only the first 100 songs of a playlist; Deezer gives them all)
- **Several downloads at once:** up to three links download side by side, each into its own playlist - a second link for the same playlist waits for the first. One notification lists them all
- **Download into a playlist:** hold a playlist's card > **Download into this playlist** opens Add songs with that playlist already picked
- **Delete all songs:** hold a playlist's card > **Delete all songs** empties it (it asks first). The playlist stays, with its picture and its YouTube link - and songs already downloaded from that YouTube playlist don't come back
- **Louder on Bluetooth, louder still:** on speakers, earbuds and the car no song is turned down any more - the boost goes on top, 12 dB to start and up to 18. The Sound page says which speaker it is playing through. Every song is levelled a little louder too
- **Artists on one line:** the player shows as many artists as fit and a **+N** for the rest - tap it to see every artist, while the ring keeps its size
- **Wrong lyrics for any song:** it's in the song menu for every song now, not only the one playing
- **Rainbow menus:** the playlist menu runs red to violet down the list with the new rows in place
- **Fixed:** a favourite song's heart showed empty when the app opened (and the first tap took the heart off); tapping the home search box left a screen of empty space instead of the playlists; the player bar flashed grey on play and pause; a grey scrollbar line showed while scrolling; a long "songs, hours" line under a playlist broke over three lines; the tagline is brighter

**New in 8.0:**

- **Song names done right:** the player shows the song's name big, with every artist underneath - "A vs. B Ft. C" lists all three, a featuring in the name moves to the artists, and YouTube clutter like "(Official Music Video)" or "[Lyrics]" drops off. Long names wrap to four lines instead of being cut off. The lock screen, notification and widget show the same
- **Remixes named properly:** an upload called "Song - Someone Remix" shows as **Song (Someone Remix)** by the song's artists, and "Song - Artists" shows the song by those artists - not the other way round
- **Louder on Bluetooth:** Sound > **Louder on Bluetooth** plays up to 12 dB louder on Bluetooth speakers, earbuds, the car and wired or USB headphones (+8 to start, 0 = off). The phone's own speaker stays as it was, and the loudest moments are held back so nothing crackles
- **An Equalizer button:** the equalizer has its own button and page on the home screen. **Sound** keeps evening out the volume, Louder on Bluetooth and the crossfade
- **Home buttons refreshed:** each in its own two tones with a soft light along the top, and the row fades out just inside its frame as you slide it, so no button runs past the edge
- **The ring:** 141 bars - 70 a side and one at the very top, so it stays mirrored - drawn at most 45 times a second, so the phone runs cooler; it moves just as fast
- **Fixed:** on phones running an accessibility service (a password manager, a voice keyboard), a tap in a playlist of thousands of songs could freeze the app for many seconds

**New in 7.0:**

- **Karaoke:** the microphone in the player opens a full-screen karaoke view - the line being sung big, each word filling in as it's sung, the next line below, and three dots counting down before the singing starts again
- **Lyrics that keep time:** lyrics from LRCLIB are now compared with the library's other uploads of the same song; one timed to a music video's intro (every line seconds late) is swapped for a well-timed one by itself - lyrics you already have are checked once, the next time their song plays. Lyrics with no times get them from the video's captions
- **Pour a YouTube playlist into one of yours:** sharing a playlist now asks where its songs go - one of your playlists, its own playlist, or a new one. Songs already there aren't downloaded again, and your playlist keeps following its own YouTube playlist
- **A new home screen:** playlists as square cards, nine to a page - swipe sideways for more - each ringed in its picture's colours, inside one frame that takes the playing song's colours
- **Menus as glowing cards.** Hold a playlist's card for its menu (Play, Shuffle, **Share playlist**, Change picture, Check YouTube, Check for wrong audio, Delete). The **...** inside a playlist is the menu for the song that's playing
- **Share a playlist** as text - its name, YouTube link and every song
- **Player bar:** back, play and next, in the song's own colours
- **The ring:** its audio bars are now spikes of glowing particles - 136 of them
- **Empty playlist folders show,** ready for songs; downloads show each song's progress
- **Big playlists stay quick:** thousands of songs draw in the background, Sort answers straight away, and songs arriving from a download never hold up a tap
- **Fixed:** deleting the song that's playing went back to the first song of the shuffle - it now goes on to the next one; menu cards could turn black while scrolling on some phones
- **Freeze log:** if the app ever stalls for more than a couple of seconds, what it was doing goes into `Carmen Musicum backup/.freezes.txt` - attach it to a bug report

**New in 6.0:**

- **Every song at about the same loudness:** each song is measured once (the way broadcasters measure loudness) and loud ones are turned down, quiet ones up - with a limiter, so nothing distorts. On unless you turn it off: home screen > **Sound**
- **Crossfade, gapless:** songs blend into each other (6 seconds to start, 0-12 in Sound). The blend starts where a song's music really ends - silent tails are skipped and the next song comes in on its first note. Next, Previous and tapping another song blend quickly instead of cutting
- **An equalizer in plain words:** Bass, Middle and Treble sliders that say what you are hearing ("More (+6 dB)", "A little less"), your phone's own presets, and every band under Advanced. Off unless you turn it on
- **Move to another playlist** (song menu): the song's file moves, and its plays and heart go with it. After the first move, **Move to** the same playlist is one tap - handy for sorting a big playlist
- **Playlist menu:** **Delete playlist** (it asks first and says how many songs), and **Check YouTube for new songs** on or off for a playlist you shared from YouTube
- **Songs YouTube will not give out** (removed, private, age-restricted) now get another upload of the same song by themselves - only when it is clearly the same recording: same name and artist, not a remix or live version, and its length within 4 seconds. Anything less certain waits under **Find another copy**
- **Wrong lyrics** in the song menu, to look the words up again by name
- **A new look:** a galaxy behind the player that looks like a real one, a fuller ring of audio bars, more stars - some knocked loose by the beat - a five-colour scheme (purple, magenta, blue, light blue, green), the bar at the bottom in the playing song's colours, and each home button in two colours of its own
- **Fixed:** the player's buttons blinked for a moment on every pause and play

**New in 5.3:**

- **Audio bars:** a ring of bars outside it moves with the music - bass at the bottom, the voice at the sides, treble at the top - and little twinkling stars pop off the bars on the hits
- **More stars, calmer details:** more twinkling stars in the cloud; the web and the sparkles are softer and easier on the eyes

**New in 5.2:**

**New in 5.1:**

- **MY MUSIC is now Carmen Musicum.** Inside, the home screen says **Repertorium**. It updates the app you have - nothing to move, and an existing `MY MUSIC backup` folder is renamed and kept
- **Search every playlist at once:** a search box on the home screen finds any song in any playlist by name or artist; tap it to play it inside its own playlist, long-press for the song menu, Back clears the search

**Install:** tap the `.apk` below on your phone, then **Install** (or **Update**).

- If Android asks, let your browser or Files app install apps.
- If Play Protect warns, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.

**First run:**

1. Allow Music and audio, and Notifications.
2. Tap **Music folder** and pick or make a folder for your music.
3. Share a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist to Carmen Musicum.

You need Android 8+ on a 64-bit ARM phone.
